About

Hi, I’m Dr. Mayra Rodríguez, a forester and certified arborist who helps communities use data, education, and collaboration to manage urban forests.

My work sits at the intersection of natural resources management and community planning. I integrate spatial data, qualitative participatory research, secondary data analysis, and mixed-methods approaches.

Whether facilitating community programs, professional trainings, or analyzing data, my goal is the same: To give people the knowledge and tools to actually participate in environmental decisions.

If you’re interested in collaborating, scroll down to see examples of my projects and products, and feel free to contact me.

Focus Areas

  • Tree benefits & ecosystem services
  • Secondary data synthesis
  • Community visioning
  • Youth engagement & co-design
  • Program development & evaluation
  • Public outreach tools
